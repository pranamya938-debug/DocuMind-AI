COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Office Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on June 15, 2026, by and between Metro Tower Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Apex Technology Solutions Inc. ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES & TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant Suite 400 located at 100 Innovation Way, Austin, Texas. The lease term shall commence on August 1, 2026, and expire on July 31, 2029 (a period of 36 calendar months).

2. RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
(a) Base Monthly Rent: Tenant shall pay Landlord a base rent of $4,500.00 per month, due and payable on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month.
(b) Late Payment Penalty: If any monthly installment is received after the fifth (5th) calendar day of the month, a mandatory late fee of 5% ($225.00) shall be immediately assessed.
(c) Security Deposit: Upon mutual execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $9,000.00 (equivalent to two months' base rent) to guarantee faithful performance of all lease covenants.

3. UTILITIES AND OPERATING EXPENSES
Tenant shall be responsible for 100% of electricity, high-speed data, and interior janitorial services. Water and municipal trash collection are included in the base rent, provided that excess consumption beyond standard office usage shall be billed directly to Tenant at municipal commercial rates.

4. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY LIMITS
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and its managing agents from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, or costs (including reasonable legal fees) arising from Tenant's occupancy, negligence, or breach of this Agreement. Tenant must maintain commercial general liability insurance with coverage limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured party. A certificate of insurance must be delivered to Landlord at least 14 business days prior to occupancy.

5. TERMINATION AND DEFAULT CONDITIONS
(a) Default: Non-payment of rent for more than 10 calendar days after written notice shall constitute an incurable event of default.
(b) Early Termination: Tenant may terminate this Agreement only after the completion of the twenty-fourth (24th) month by providing at least 90 days' prior written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to three (3) months' rent ($13,500.00).

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the authorized representatives have executed this Lease Agreement as of the date first above written.
